### Where Does "Why Not" Come From?

For decades in corporate environments, I’d sit in meetings listening to endless debate and think, _"Let's just build it... why not?"_ But as a good corporate soldier, I’d nod and say, _"Let's dig into that and build a business case."_

Now that I no longer report to corporate overlords, **"Why Not?"** is the primary justification for everything I explore, build, and write.

### Advertising Philosophy

I hate invasive ads as much as you do—especially tracking scripts that follow your search history across the web to serve target placements. Whenever I spot that level of tracking, I actively avoid those products and sites.

Since ads are the standard way to cover hosting bills on a technical blog, I stick to two ground rules:

1. **Zero Ads Initially:** No advertising will run on this site until traffic reaches a meaningful scale (tens of thousands of page views per month).
2. **Privacy-First Networks:** If I ever reach that threshold, I will exclusively use privacy-focused, contextual ad networks like **EthicalAds**—never tracking scripts or behavioral ad networks.
